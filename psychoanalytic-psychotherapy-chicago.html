Psychoanalytic Psychotherapy in Chicago

A deeper form of therapy for adults.

Nicole Glass, MA, LCPC offers psychoanalytic psychotherapy and psychodynamic therapy in Chicago for adults seeking a deeper understanding of anxiety, depression, relationship patterns, self-worth, identity, shame, grief, longing, conflict, and self-protection.

Psychoanalytic treatment follows what comes forward in speech, including what may feel uncertain, repetitive, or not yet fully understood. Over time, this can help patterns become clearer and support reflection, change, and a more complex understanding of oneself.

Treatment

Psychodynamic and psychoanalytic care.

This work is attentive to recurring ways of relating, internal conflict, protective forms of coping, and experiences that may be carried or repeated over time.

Therapy is not limited to symptom relief. It can also support reflection on identity, desire, loss, self-esteem, work, family history, and the complexity of relationships.

Patients are invited to speak as freely as possible about what comes to mind. This may include memories, dreams, worries, silences, contradictions, frustrations, fantasies, bodily states, and thoughts that feel minor until they begin to repeat.

The aim is not to offer quick advice or a predetermined interpretation. The work develops through careful listening, attention to language, and a sustained interest in how a person's present life is shaped by meanings that may not yet be fully known.

Beginning Treatment

The first conversation begins the work of listening.
