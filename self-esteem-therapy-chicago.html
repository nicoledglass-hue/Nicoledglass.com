Self-Esteem Therapy in Chicago

For shame, self-criticism, and the pressure to be enough.

Difficulties with self-esteem may show up as harsh self-judgment, achievement pressure, difficulty receiving care, fear of failure, or the feeling that approval never settles into a lasting sense of worth.

Therapy can help examine the internal standards, early experiences, relational patterns, and cultural expectations that shape how a person comes to regard the self.

Treatment Focus

Self-worth is often formed in relationship.

A person may appear capable and accomplished while privately feeling fraudulent, inadequate, unseen, or difficult to care for. These feelings can shape work, intimacy, ambition, disappointment, and the ability to rest.

Psychoanalytic treatment makes room to consider shame, pride, criticism, desire, envy, dependency, and the ways a person may protect against vulnerability. The aim is not a slogan of confidence, but a more complex and durable understanding of oneself.
