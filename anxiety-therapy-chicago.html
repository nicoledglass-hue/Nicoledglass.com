Anxiety Therapy in Chicago

When worry, tension, or dread becomes difficult to set down.

Anxiety may appear as overthinking, restlessness, irritability, difficulty sleeping, fear of disappointing others, or a sense of danger that does not have a clear source.

In psychodynamic and psychoanalytic therapy, anxiety is approached as something that has meaning. Treatment can help clarify what anxiety may be protecting against, what it may be trying to communicate, and how it is connected to relationships, history, desire, and conflict.

Treatment Focus

Anxiety can be listened to, not only managed.

Some people come to therapy after living for years with a mind that does not easily settle. Others notice anxiety most sharply in work, intimacy, family obligations, transitions, or the pressure to appear composed.

Therapy offers a consistent setting to speak about worry, shame, anger, longing, avoidance, perfectionism, and the ways a person may try to remain safe. Over time, patterns can become more visible and less automatic.
