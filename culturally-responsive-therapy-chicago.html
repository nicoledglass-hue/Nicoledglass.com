Culturally Responsive Therapy in Chicago

Therapy attentive to identity, culture, and self-understanding.

Nicole Glass, MA, LCPC offers culturally responsive psychotherapy in Chicago for adults navigating anxiety, depression, self-worth, relationships, racial and cultural identity, and the emotional impact of social and professional life.

In psychoanalytic and psychodynamic therapy, identity and culture are not treated as separate from emotional experience. They can shape how a person understands desire, conflict, protection, intimacy, visibility, and self-understanding.

Identity-Informed Care

A reflective approach to what may be hard to name.

Treatment can attend to questions of identity, family history, race, culture, work, relationships, and self-worth without reducing a person's experience to a single category.

This approach supports adults who want to understand recurring patterns and the meanings that emerge through speaking, reflection, and sustained attention over time.
