Depression Therapy in Chicago

When vitality, interest, or meaning feels muted.

Depression may involve sadness, emptiness, fatigue, withdrawal, irritability, or a sense that ordinary life has become difficult to enter.

Psychoanalytic and psychodynamic therapy can attend to depression as more than a symptom to remove. It can open inquiry into grief, anger, disappointment, attachment, loss, self-criticism, and the parts of life that may feel unavailable.

Treatment Focus

Depression often carries a history.

Some forms of depression are visible through tears or withdrawal. Others are quieter: functioning well outwardly while feeling flat, distant, ashamed, or unable to want very much.

Treatment provides a place to speak without rushing toward a forced optimism. The work may involve listening closely to grief, anger, inhibition, longing, and the meanings that have gathered around loss, desire, and self-worth.
